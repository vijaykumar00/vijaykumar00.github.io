About me

Hey there! A little about me.

A developer from Mohali, Punjab, who loves building useful things and then heading off to the mountains.

Hey, I am Vijay! I studied computer science, and for the last 5+ years I have been building websites and web apps for a living, mostly with PHP and Laravel. I still get a kick out of turning a messy, manual process into something that just works.

I have built online stores for jewellery brands, dashboards for a healthcare company, payment systems for real estate and a bunch of custom tools in between. What I enjoy most is sitting with people, understanding how their work actually runs, and then building software that makes their day a little easier.

When I am not coding, you will probably find me on a trail somewhere, planning my next trip, or singing along with my guitar. I believe the best ideas show up when you step away from the screen for a while.

What I love building

  • Websites and web apps that turn everyday business work into simple, reliable tools.
  • Online stores with real inventory, smooth checkouts and payments that just work.
  • Dashboards, portals and booking or CRM systems shaped around how people actually work.
  • Connecting APIs, payments and databases into one clean Laravel platform.
